## Observed facts

### Homepage, wide

- The header is one even bar. Short nav words sit on the left at one size. The wordmark is centered. Language sits on the right. “Dịch vụ” is a nav word, always visible, the same size as the other nav words.
- The first band is a wide photograph with comfortable side margins, not a stack of mismatched blocks.
- Under it, a small-caps line, then one serif title. The title is large, but it sits in the same left measure as the text under it. The next photographs share that page width.

### Services page

- Wide: a room photograph with the word “Dịch vụ” centered on it, then a centered title and a short paragraph. Featured topics are photo cards, three across: image, name, one short line, “Xem chi tiết”. No price on those cards. Further down, each tier is a wide band: photograph and text share the row, the price is the loud figure, the duration sits with the price. The photograph swaps sides from row to row.
- Phone 390: the header becomes MENU, the wordmark, and language. Featured cards become one column in the same order (photo, name, one line, link). A menu band stacks photo then text. Name and price share one row when the name is short.

### Detail page (phone, same URL)

- Order: short “Dịch vụ” hero, offering name centered, one short line, price and minute range on one line, then the photograph, then a long step list, then related items, then a way back. No minute-chip form. The name, the price line, and the photograph share one column, so none of them is a different width from the others.

## Interpretations

- The calm comes from one measure. A title, a price, and a photograph are sized to the same column. Nothing is a poster next to a caption.
- “Dịch vụ” is findable because it is a nav word at the same size as the rest, and the services page opens with the menu of topics before the long list.
- Their gold header, lotus drawing, booking button, city chips, and large corner radius are that brand.

## Recommendations for Jade

- Keep Jade’s Ivory, Forest, Sand, Bronze hairline, Cormorant, Manrope, and the existing photos and prices.
- Give the services block a visible row — Massage, Gội đầu dưỡng sinh, Combo — so the menu is on the page, not only behind a hover.
- Keep offer cards the same height: one photo ratio, a title that occupies the same slot, a short sample line, “Xem chi tiết”.
- On an intent list, keep the photograph modest and put the name and the “từ” price on one baseline.
- On a detail page, center one column: name, the labeled sample line, minutes beside the real price, the photograph, the sample steps, related names. Switching minutes stays, as a quiet row, because Jade has several real prices.
