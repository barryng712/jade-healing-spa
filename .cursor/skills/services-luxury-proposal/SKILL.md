# Services luxury proposal

Read `.cursor/skills/design-analyze/SKILL.md` first, then the open CR’s BA locks. Do not implement `app/`, `components/`, or `lib/`.

## Translation

Reference rhythm — photo scale, quiet paper, a price the eye can scan, wide alternation, a phone stack — becomes a Jade proposal. Use tokens already in `docs/design-guidelines.md`, frames in `lib/photos.ts`, and minutes and `formatPrice` from `lib/menu.ts`.

Every group row links to the existing detail URL. Every minute mark shows its own price. Wide alternation, if chosen, is wide screens only. Below `lg`, stack photo, then name, then prices.

Name the real components (`service-catalog`, `service-group-list`, `service-detail-stage`, `service-ritual`, `photo-frame`) and the exact type size, line-height, padding, gap, and photo ratio. Do not leave band versus card, or the phone spacing, open.

## Do not

Clone the reference: no gold-on-black header, botanical drawings, pill buttons, booking, city filter, their sentences, prices, inclusions, or images. No new palette, no shadow, no stock photo, no invented marketing sentence. No “from” price. No change to the home services band or the header.

## Example

Their gold header and lotus drawing stay out. A wide photo and a Forest serif name stay in.
